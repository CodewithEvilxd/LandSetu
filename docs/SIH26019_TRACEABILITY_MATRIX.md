# SIH26019 Requirements Traceability Matrix
## National Digital Platform for Research, Policy Innovation, and Evidence-Based Land Governance

**Auditor:** Antigravity IDE Compliance Auditor & Senior Full-Stack Architect  
**Evaluation Standard:** SIH26019 Official Problem Statement (Ministry of Rural Development, Department of Land Resources)  
**Verification Date:** September 2026  
**Status Legend:**
- **PASS**: Fully implemented with verifiable code, active routes, genuine database records, and automated test coverage.
- **PARTIAL**: Architecture and interfaces active, but dataset coverage or regional depth is currently representative/pilot.
- **MISSING**: Requirement not implemented or awaiting external authoritative data feed.
- **FAKE/UNSUPPORTED**: Hardcoded claims without database backing (Audit rule: Flagged and sanitized).
- **DRIFT**: Functionality drifting into an operational land registry or generic portal rather than research/policy intelligence.
